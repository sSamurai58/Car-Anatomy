## Content pillars

1. **Powertrains** — NA V8, supercharged, twin-turbo, hybrid boost, EV drive units  
2. **Drivetrain** — dual-clutch, auto, AWD vs RWD, limited-slip  
3. **Chassis & brakes** — suspension geometry, aero, carbon-ceramic brakes  
4. **Electronics** — ECU/TCU, launch control, traction modes  
5. **Icons & anatomy** — one famous US/sportscar model, one system explained  

**Rule:** one Short = one idea. Hook → one mechanism → one takeaway → end screen.
